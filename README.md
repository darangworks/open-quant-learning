# Open Quant Learning

Persian educational content on systematic trading research — explained
in plain language, backed by real research.

This repository is the education layer of the Open Quant Research
program. Articles here are not investment advice, and they are not
signal services. They explain how research on systematic trading
strategies actually works — using real projects, including negative
results.

## Articles

### 001 — چرا یک بک‌تست سودده کافی نیست؟

A story-driven case study: how I reconstructed a kNN trading
indicator, found two causality-related flaws, corrected them, and
ended up with a negative result.

- **Language:** Persian
- **Source:** `posts/001-backtest-is-not-enough.fa.md`
- **Underlying research:** [Causal kNN Crypto Strategy](https://github.com/darangworks/causal-knn-crypto-strategy)

---

## Purpose

Most quantitative trading content online focuses on *what strategy
made money*. This repository focuses on *how to know whether a
result is trustworthy*.

The articles here are based on actual research projects, published
openly, including results that were negative.
